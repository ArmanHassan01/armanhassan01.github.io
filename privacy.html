Privacy

Website analytics

This portfolio may use Google Analytics 4 to understand general website traffic and engagement. Analytics is used for portfolio improvement and research-outreach attribution.

Information that may be measured

  • Page views and engagement
  • Approximate geographic area such as country, region, or city when available
  • Device/browser information
  • Traffic and referral source
  • Campaign parameters in portfolio links (UTM parameters)
  • Clicks on the CV, publications, email, projects, and social links

What this site does not intentionally collect

The portfolio does not intentionally send visitor names, email addresses, form contents, exact physical addresses, custom IP logs, or browser fingerprints to analytics.
